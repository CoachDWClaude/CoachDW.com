# BodyStats: draft spec

Status: waiting on Danny to confirm the fields and which tier gets what.

## Job

One place for a member's body numbers, linked to the check-in, so they can see what moves their lifts.

## Proposed fields

From the check-in: sleep, feel, days since last session, notes.

New, all optional: bodyweight, waist, sleep hours, protein, hydration, food quality, calories.

## Rules

- Enter each number once. A number given in the check-in appears in BodyStats, and bodyweight also updates the dips and pull ups load.
- Show trends, not daily verdicts. Weekly averages for bodyweight.
- Everything optional, with a way to hide any number.
- Plain data on the phone first. Anything stored on a server is health data and needs privacy terms first.

## Links to other features

- Progress graph: adds new numbers to its What Changed panel.
- Food log: feeds protein and calories.
- Membership: the October plan puts the daily check-in graphs in the Mighty 300. To be decided.
